# Obsidian Frequency — Short Form Scripts
## Batch 08 — Week of Oct 13–19 (4 shorts, V08 live Tue Oct 13)

**Note (v4.0 rework):** the long-form faction videos (08–10) deliberately
use zero Midjourney imagery — pure document design, per their own
production notes. Shorts don't inherit that restriction. These four
shorts use a new recurring location — Directorate Headquarters, exterior
and interior — built once and reused with a different accent color per
faction week (cool grey-blue for Wardens, amber for Architects, purple
for Threshold Initiative), giving short-form its own visual identity
distinct from the long-form's document-only approach.
