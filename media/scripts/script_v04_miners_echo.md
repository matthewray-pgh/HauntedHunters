# Script Notes

## Runtime Target
8–10 minutes | ~875–1025 words of narration

*(Expanded from the original 6–7 min / ~650–800 words per the Video 04
script-expansion patch — ~225 words of new narration added across four
insertion points in Sections 1, 2, 3 and the closing reflection.
Estimated runtime after expansion ~8:00–8:45 at existing pacing, before
final BEAT tuning. All inserts hold the quiet, restrained register the
notes call for — no new urgency. No new visual assets; the single
document system and single full-frame moment are unchanged.)*

## Complexity
2/5 per video-pipeline.md — document visuals + ambient sound design +
narration. Deliberately the lightest production in the arc so far. One
visual system only. Let that be a relief after Video 03, not a step down —
this file earns its restraint from what it's about: an entity that does
nothing except repeat itself.

## Voice Direction
Same Archivist treatment as Videos 01–03 — ElevenLabs, tape recorder
playback, slight wow and flutter, subtle hiss, narrow frequency range.
This file should sound quieter than Video 03, not more urgent. The subject
is a sound-sensitive entity; the narration itself should feel aware of
that irony without ever stating it outright. Pacing is slower than Videos
01–03 — more space between lines than usual, even outside marked beats.

## Visual Approach
One visual system, kept simple per the complexity rating:

- **[DOCUMENT]** — the HH-016 entity classification file. Same treatment
  as Videos 01–03. Typewriter font, aged paper, CRT overlay, Ken Burns pan.

**[FULL FRAME]** used once — the loop reconstruction. Per house style,
still the single moment per video allowed to fill the entire screen.
No second visual system (no surveillance insert, no testimony cards) —
this video does not need one, and adding one would work against the
complexity-2 rating.

## On-Screen Text
No on-screen Archivist text during narration. Closing annotation card
only, per house convention.

## Silence Note
[BEAT] — minimum two seconds.
[LONG BEAT] — minimum four seconds.
This video should feel like it has more room to breathe than Videos
01–03, even without a testimony-card-style enforced silence system.
Let beats run slightly long rather than short if in doubt.

## Image Transition Note
Document visuals: slow dissolves, minimum four seconds hold, matching
house standard. The Full Frame loop reconstruction: dissolve in (this is
not recovered footage like Video 02's surveillance insert — it is a
reconstruction, and should feel authored, not found), hold in total
silence, dissolve back out.

## Continuity Requirement
Mining ambient (distant dripping water, structural creaks, deep cave
echo) must be the exact same audio asset used in Video 03 — first
established there, reused here for the audience to recognize
immediately. Do not resource or regenerate this asset. See
`docs/production/asset_sourcing_guide.md`.
