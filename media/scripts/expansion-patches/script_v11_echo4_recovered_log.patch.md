# Video 11 — Recovered Log: Echo-4
## STRUCTURAL EXPANSION MEMO — apply against script_v11_echo4_recovered_log.md

**This is not a word-count patch.** Per the runtime update, Video 11 is
carved out as a special case: it has zero Archivist narration in its
body, so "write to word count" doesn't apply here. The levers for this
video are dialogue beats, [DEAD AIR] holds, and comms exchange length —
not prose.

**Note on source material:** I have this script's full Script Notes
(voice direction, continuity requirements, silence system, First
Phantom seed spec) but not the scene-by-scene dialogue script itself.
This memo identifies *where* to add material structurally, not exact
new lines — actual dialogue should be written by whoever is closest to
the three Hunter voices' established rhythm, then checked against the
constraints below.

**What is NOT touched, under any circumstance:**
- **Zero narration anywhere in the body.** This is called out as "the
  single most important check for this specific video." Do not add
  Archivist voiceover to hit runtime — every new beat must be dialogue,
  silence, or degraded audio.
- **Echo-4's outcome stays unconfirmed in every particular** except the
  fact that the Red Platform is reached — no line should explain what
  it is, what happened to the team afterward, or resolve the mystery.
- **The recovered photograph's background figure stays genuinely
  ambiguous** — three-second hold, never referenced in dialogue, never
  revisited. Do not add a second reference to it anywhere.
- **The exact reused phantom-train audio asset** (from Videos 02 and 06)
  and its behavior — untouched.
- **[DEAD AIR] markers are the backbone of this video's tension** — per
  the script's own note, "do not compress under any circumstance." New
  [DEAD AIR] beats should be added, not have existing ones stretched.
- **Echo-4 Three's restraint** — "do not overwrite this role with more
  dialogue than necessary." Any new dialogue for this character should
  stay minimal; if anything, give her fewer new lines than the other two.

**Where new runtime is added (three structural levers, not scripted
lines):**

---

### LEVER 1 — Extend the opening comms-check exchange with the Dispatcher
The Dispatcher currently appears "only in the opening comms check" before
contact is lost. Consider extending this opening exchange by one or two
additional procedural back-and-forth beats — call sign confirmation,
equipment check, a second status ping — before the team enters the
tunnels. This is the one place in the video where dialogue can be
purely procedural and unhurried without needing to carry tension, since
everything is still normal at this point. Estimated addition: 20-30
seconds of screen time.

**Constraint:** keep this exchange as flat and institutional as
everything else involving the Dispatcher — do not use it to
foreshadow what's coming. The mundanity is the point; the horror hasn't
started yet.

---

### LEVER 2 — Add one additional [DEAD AIR] beat mid-recording
The script currently uses [DEAD AIR] (5-8 seconds of near-total silence)
at key tension points. Consider adding one additional instance of this
marker somewhere between the initial signs of trouble (Echo-4 Two's
composure starting to slip) and the approach to the Red Platform itself
— a moment where the team stops communicating entirely for a stretch,
before resuming. This is the single highest-value lever for this video
specifically, since the script's own notes treat silence as the primary
tension mechanism, not dialogue density.

**Constraint:** place it where it would organically occur — not as a
scheduled pause, but where a real recording might have a gap (equipment
trouble, someone signaling for quiet, disorientation). Should feel like
something is happening in the silence, not like the tape is just
running.

---

### LEVER 3 — Extend the approach to the Red Platform with one additional
navigation/environment exchange
Before the team reaches the Red Platform, consider one additional short
exchange between Echo-4 Two (navigation/equipment) and Echo-4 Actual —
readings that don't make sense, a corridor that doesn't match the map,
equipment behaving strangely. This should escalate the "something is
wrong" feeling without resolving into an explanation. Estimated
addition: 30-45 seconds.

**Constraint:** Echo-4 Three should not gain new dialogue here per the
"restraint" note above — if a third voice is needed in this exchange,
let her silence itself be part of the beat (she's asked something and
doesn't answer, or answers with fewer words than expected) rather than
giving her new lines.

---

## Net Effect

- **Estimated total added runtime:** roughly 1:30-2:15 across the three
