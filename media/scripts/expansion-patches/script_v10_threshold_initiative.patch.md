**Purpose:** raise runtime from 6-7 min (~650-780 words) to the new
8-10 min standard (~900-1000 words). Highest priority flag (faction
trilogy) — this is also the video that closes the shared-signatory
thread, so extra care is needed around what stays unsaid.

**Source material:** I have this script's Pre-Roll, Document Open, and
Sections 1-2 (What the Threshold Initiative Believes / Why Adaptation
Is Their Position) verbatim, plus the Content Outline structure. The
two insertions below extend those two verbatim sections directly.

**What is NOT touched — read before anything else, this video carries
the most sensitive constraints in the trilogy:**
- **No line anywhere states the "one founder" conclusion outright.**
  Per the script's own note: *"the closest the script comes is the
  Archivist noticing the pattern and refusing to finish the thought out
  loud... over-explaining it in editing would undo three videos of
  setup."* Neither insertion below closes this gap — both stop short,
  consistent with the existing pattern.
- **The endgame/danger-assessment section stays deliberately shorter or
  absent**, per the outline's "implied, never stated" instruction. Do
  not use the new word-count budget to fill this section in — the
  insertions below extend Sections 1-2 instead, leaving the thin
  endgame section exactly as thin as designed.
- **The Veil Contamination visual element's spread rate and constraints**
  — never resolves into a shape or face, spreads slower and subtler than
  Video 05's static. Untouched.
- **The shared-signatory seed, third and final appearance** — identical
  spec to Videos 08-09. No narration references it.
- Voice direction: this is the first video where the Archivist should
  sound genuinely unsettled rather than restrained or clinical — small
  hesitations are appropriate here for the first time in the arc. New
  material should lean into that shift, not away from it.

**Where new words are added (two insertion points — kept to two rather
than three, since this video's tightest constraint is what must stay
