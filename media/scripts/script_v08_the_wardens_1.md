# OBSIDIAN FREQUENCY
## Video 08 — The Wardens
### Production Script v1.0 — Faction Dossier / CapCut Pipeline

---

# Script Notes

## Runtime Target
6–7 minutes | ~650–780 words of narration

## Complexity
2/5 per video-pipeline.md.

## One Job
Introduce the first Directorate faction. The Wardens believe the Veil
must be sealed permanently, at any cost. Establish that the organization
has an ideological core, not just an operational one — this video and
its two successors (Videos 09, 10) exist specifically so each faction
gets full focus rather than a rushed three-way split.

## Continuity Requirements — Read Before Writing Any Further Draft
This is the **first of three faction videos that share one seeded
thread**, per `first-phantom-thread-seed-map.md`:

- Wardens charter document contains a redacted signatory block. Videos
  09 (The Architects) and 10 (The Threshold Initiative) will each show
  their own charter with **the exact same redacted signatory — same
  length, same position on the page, same color treatment.** The eventual
  reveal (post-Video 10) is that all three factions share one founder.
- **This script defines the physical spec that Videos 09 and 10 must
  match exactly** — see the Charter Insert section below. Do not deviate
  from these measurements when those scripts are written, and flag this
  spec explicitly to whoever builds the Video 09/10 document assets.
- Do not name or describe the Architects or Threshold Initiative directly
  in this script beyond acknowledging that other factions exist — each
  gets its own dedicated introduction in its own video.

## Voice Direction
Same Archivist treatment as Videos 01–07 — ElevenLabs, tape recorder
playback, slight wow and flutter, subtle hiss, narrow frequency range.
This file should sound different from the entity/incident files — less
dread, more weary institutional caution. The Archivist is not afraid of
the Wardens. The Archivist is being careful about how much to say about
them, which is a different register than fear.

## Visual Approach
Single visual system, and — for the first time in the arc — no
Midjourney-generated entity or location imagery at all. This is
intentional: faction videos are bureaucratic horror, not environmental
or creature horror. Everything on screen is a designed document asset,
built in Canva, not generated in Midjourney.

- **[DOCUMENT]** — the Wardens internal intelligence assessment /
  faction dossier. Same base treatment as prior documents — typewriter
  font, aged paper, CRT overlay, Ken Burns pan — but with a more
  institutional, less archival visual character (crisper paper, less
  wear) reflecting that this is a current internal document, not a
  recovered historical one.
- **[ORG CHART INSERT]** — partially visible organizational chart,
  most of the chart blacked out or cropped by the frame edge, showing
  only the fragment relevant to the Wardens' position within the
  Directorate.
- **[CHARTER INSERT]** — a stapled sub-page containing the Wardens'
  founding charter, carrying the shared redacted-signatory seed (see
  below).

No Full Frame moment this video — per the complexity-2 rating and the
"no imagery" approach, this video does not use the house Full Frame
convention. The document itself carries the entire runtime.

## On-Screen Text
No on-screen Archivist text during narration. Closing annotation card
only.

## Silence Note
[BEAT] — minimum two seconds.
[LONG BEAT] — minimum four seconds.

## First Phantom Seed — Locked Spec, Reused in Videos 09 and 10
The Wardens charter (stapled sub-page within the dossier) includes a
founding declaration line with a signatory beneath it, fully redacted.
**Exact spec, to be matched precisely by Videos 09 and 10:**

- Position: bottom third of the charter page, directly beneath the
  founding declaration paragraph, left-aligned with the paragraph's
  margin.
- Redaction bar dimensions: width equivalent to a standard two-word
  name at the document's body type size (approximately the width of
  "REDACTED NAME" if it were printed, roughly 90px at standard document
  scale) — same width across all three videos, no variation.
- Color treatment: cold blue-grey, distinct from the black redaction
  bars used everywhere else in the arc (Video 02's crossed-out line,
  Video 05's live redaction bars). **No hex value for this color
  currently exists in `docs/visual-style-guide.md`.** Recommend adding
  one before this asset is built — suggested value `#5A6470` (cool
  slate blue-grey, desaturated enough to sit quietly against the
  existing palette without competing with the Veil purple). Confirm
  before production and then treat as locked for Videos 09 and 10 as
  well.
- Camera treatment: passes over at the same speed as the rest of the
  page, no zoom, no lingering, no narration reference at any point in
  this script.

## Image Transition Note
Document dissolves: three seconds in, three seconds out, house standard.
Org chart and charter inserts: same dissolve convention, not a hard cut
— these are official documents being read, not recovered fragments or
seeds requiring the harder cut language used elsewhere.
