# Script Notes

## Runtime Target
7–8 minutes | ~750–900 words of narration

## Complexity
3/5 per video-pipeline.md.

## One Job
Pay off the Station 13 thread seeded in Video 02. Introduce The Red
Platform as a mystery held for Video 11 — do not resolve it here.

## Continuity Requirements — Read Before Writing Any Further Draft
This video sits directly downstream of Video 02 and must not contradict
it:

- Video 02 states explicitly: *"Their deployment orders are in this
  archive. Their outcome is not."* This video shows Echo-4's deployment
  orders and the fragmented recordings recovered from the incident —
  **it must not reveal what ultimately happened to Echo-4.** That
  payoff is reserved for Video 11 (Recovered Log: Echo-4). If this
  script drifts toward answering it, cut the line.
- Video 02's handwritten note — *"Do not board the final train."* — and
  its phantom train approach sound are both meant to recur here. Per
  `docs/production/asset_sourcing_guide.md`, the exact same edited
  phantom-train audio file used in Video 02 must be reused identically
  in this video (and again in Video 11) — do not regenerate or re-edit
  it. This is a deliberate conditioning effect across the three videos.
- Primary entity is HH-013 (The Conductor) — cross-reference it, don't
  re-explain it. The audience already has that file.
- Transit ambient must be the same base layer established in Video 02,
  not a new asset.

## Voice Direction
Same Archivist treatment as Videos 01–05 — ElevenLabs, tape recorder
playback, slight wow and flutter, subtle hiss, narrow frequency range.
This is a payoff video for a thread the audience has been waiting on
since Video 02 — the Archivist should sound aware of that, almost
reluctant to finally open this file, rather than eager to reveal it.

## Visual Approach
Three visual elements, two of which are established systems reused
rather than new ones:

- **[DOCUMENT]** — the Station 13 incident report. Same treatment as
  Video 03. Typewriter font, aged paper, CRT overlay, Ken Burns pan.
- **[AUDIO FRAGMENT — FULL FRAME]** — reuses Video 02's Section 5
  convention exactly: document fades out, near-black full frame holds,
  degraded audio plays with zero narration under it, document dissolves
  back in after. This is where the fragmented comms recording plays.
- **[LOCATION INSERT — RED PLATFORM MAPS]** — same insert convention as
  Video 03/05 (aged document reference, camera holds, no elaborate
  camera movement). Unlike Video 05's facility map, this insert is
  allowed a narration line around it — the Red Platform is an
  acknowledged open mystery, not a hidden seed the audience has to spot.

No VHS surveillance/glitch-cut system this video — that's reserved for
Video 02/11's surveillance footage specifically, not general incident
reports.

## On-Screen Text
No on-screen Archivist text during narration. Closing annotation card
only.

## Silence Note
[BEAT] — minimum two seconds.
[LONG BEAT] — minimum four seconds.

## First Phantom Seed — Read Carefully Before Audio Production
The recovered comms audio fragment contains a subsonic frequency
component — approximately 0.3 seconds in duration, occurring twice
within the fragment. It must sit below or at the edge of comfortable
audibility — present in the mix, not narratively pointed at. **The
on-screen "Directorate analysis" document text must NOT flag or
identify this frequency** — the written analysis in the document
describes the audio fragment in purely surface terms (garbled, partial,
inconclusive) and says nothing about a subsonic anomaly. This is a
sound-design task as much as a script one — flag it clearly for whoever
builds the ElevenLabs/audio pass, since it's easy to lose in a text-only
read of this document.

## Image Transition Note
Document dissolves: three seconds in, three seconds out, house standard.
Audio Fragment Full Frame: matches Video 02's convention — dissolve in,
hold in near-black for the full audio duration, dissolve back out. Red
Platform map insert: same dissolve treatment as Video 03/05's document
inserts — this one does not need the harder "no dissolve" treatment used
for silent First Phantom seeds, since it's an acknowledged mystery, not
a hidden one.
