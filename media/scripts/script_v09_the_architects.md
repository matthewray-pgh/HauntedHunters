# Script Notes

## Runtime Target
8–10 minutes | ~815–945 words of narration

## Complexity
2/5 per video-pipeline.md.

## One Job
Introduce the faction that caused Ashfall. Recontextualize Video 05 —
the experiments weren't rogue. They were policy.

## Continuity Requirements — Read Before Writing Any Further Draft
This video does two things that must land exactly right:

1. **It confirms, not implies, that Ashfall was Architects policy.**
   Video 05's closing annotation states: *"Someone approved this. I
   haven't found who. Yet."* This video answers that — but only at the
   policy level, not the individual level. The Architects authorized
   Ashfall as doctrine. The specific person who signed off remains
   exactly as unknown as before — **do not name or reveal an individual
   here.** The recontextualization is institutional (a faction did this
   on purpose) not personal (here's the person responsible). Keep those
   two facts distinct in every draft pass.
2. **It reuses the exact shared-signatory seed spec locked in
   `script_v08_the_wardens.md`.** Same position (bottom third of the
   charter page, beneath the founding declaration, left-aligned with
   the paragraph margin), same redaction bar width (~90px, two-word-name
   equivalent), same color (cold blue-grey, `#5A6470` pending style
   guide confirmation). **Do not vary any of these measurements from
   Video 08.** The audience is meant to recognize this immediately as
   the same redaction, not a similar one — any drift undermines the
   entire seed.

## Voice Direction
Same Archivist treatment as Videos 01–08 — ElevenLabs, tape recorder
playback, slight wow and flutter, subtle hiss, narrow frequency range.
Where Video 08's Wardens file was delivered with weary caution, this one
should carry something closer to quiet anger held in check — the
Archivist already told the audience someone approved Ashfall and
couldn't find who. Finding the "who" at the policy level, four videos
later, should land as validation of that earlier frustration, not a
neutral fact.

## Visual Approach
Same structural approach as Video 08 — single document system, no
Midjourney-generated entity or location imagery. This continues the
established faction-video visual language (bureaucratic horror through
document design only) while using a different accent treatment to keep
the two factions visually distinct:

- **[DOCUMENT]** — the Architects internal intelligence assessment.
  Same crisp, current-document treatment as Video 08 (less archival
  wear than recovered historical files), but accented in warm amber
  `#8C6E4A` rather than the Wardens' cooler institutional look — using
  an already-locked palette color rather than introducing a new one.
- **[ASHFALL CROSS-REFERENCE INSERT]** — a document excerpt explicitly
  linking back to Video 05's project file, presented as an internal
  citation within this assessment rather than a new incident report.
- **[CHARTER INSERT]** — the Architects' founding charter, carrying the
  shared redacted-signatory seed, spec locked to Video 08 exactly.

No Full Frame moment this video, consistent with Video 08 and the
complexity-2 rating.

**Production note for Video 10:** if the Threshold Initiative also
receives a distinct accent color when that script is written, consider
the Veil purple `#5F4B6E` for thematic fit (integration with the Veil),
but that decision belongs to Video 10's script, not this one — flagging
here only so the three factions read as a deliberately differentiated
set once all three exist.

## On-Screen Text
No on-screen Archivist text during narration. Closing annotation card
only.

## Silence Note
[BEAT] — minimum two seconds.
[LONG BEAT] — minimum four seconds.

## First Phantom Seed — Must Match Video 08 Exactly
See Continuity Requirements above. This is the seed's second of three
appearances. The narration should not call attention to the repetition
— the visual recognition is the payoff, not a verbal one. Do not add a
line like "this looks familiar" or similar — let the audience notice on
their own.

## Image Transition Note
Document dissolves: three seconds in, three seconds out, house standard,
matching Video 08. No hard cuts in this video.
