# Script Notes

## Runtime Target
8–10 minutes | ~825–955 words of narration

*(Expanded from the original 6–7 min / ~650–780 words per the Video 12
script-expansion patch — ~175 words of new narration added across three
insertion points in Sections 1, 3, and the Section 4 lead-in. Estimated
runtime after expansion ~7:45–8:30 at existing pacing, before the
extended eight-second final-page hold and ten-second closing annotation,
both of which push it further into the 8–10 min band.)*

## Complexity
2/5 per video-pipeline.md.

## One Job
Show the audience the shape of the cover-up itself. The Veil wasn't
discovered in 1968. It was re-discovered. Something existed before the
Directorate.

## Canon Decision — Now Locked
The pre-1968 date required for this video's blank timeline entry has
been chosen and locked: **1919**. This is recorded in
`canon/timeline/master_timeline.md` and `docs/ai-agent-handoff.md`. Per
the outline's production notes, no content is assigned to this date
within Arc 1 — the video shows the date itself, with nothing beside it.
What actually happened in 1919 is reserved for Arc 2 and must not be
implied, hinted at, or explained in this script beyond what's written
below.

## Continuity Requirements — Read Before Writing Any Further Draft
This video's three inconsistencies are pulled directly from language
already established in Videos 01, 03, and 05 — re-examined, not
reinvented. Do not paraphrase these source lines; quote them exactly as
they appear in their original scripts, since the entire argument of this
video depends on the audience recognizing language they've already
heard:

1. **Video 03** — the incident report calls the Black Vein structure
   *"Inconsistent with known geological formations."* Re-examined here:
   known to whom, in 1968, before the Directorate had any research base
   of its own to compare against?
2. **Video 03** — the second testimony card: *"It was already there
   before we broke through."* This is the line's **third callback**
   across the arc — first as the original testimony (Video 03), second
   echoed in Short 01's "It was already here," and now directly
   re-examined for its literal meaning: it predates the mine. It
   predates 1968.
3. **Video 01** — the orientation document's definition: *"The Veil is
   the hidden layer beneath stable human reality. It is not a separate
   universe. Not an afterlife. Not an alternate dimension in any
   traditional sense."* Re-examined here: that is a settled, textbook-
   precise definition, delivered with total confidence, from an
   organization barely months old at the time this document was
   written. Definitions that clean are not usually developed that fast.
4. **Video 05** — Project Ashfall's stated goal: *"controlled interaction
   with the Veil."* Referenced more briefly in Section 3 — controlled
   implies familiarity. You don't control what you've never encountered
   before.

## Voice Direction
Same Archivist treatment as Videos 01–11 — ElevenLabs, tape recorder
playback, slight wow and flutter, subtle hiss, narrow frequency range.
Per the outline, the closing annotation is **the most agitated moment
of Arc 1** — more than Video 10's Threshold Initiative close. Unlike
Video 10, where the agitation stayed mostly beneath a controlled
surface, this video can let a little more through in the body of the
narration itself, not just the closing card — the Archivist has spent
eleven videos being careful, and this is the video where "careful"
starts visibly costing something.

## Visual Approach
Single document system, no Midjourney-generated imagery, consistent
with Videos 08–10's institutional pattern:

- **[DOCUMENT]** — the Directorate master timeline, a multi-page
  document. Same crisp, current-document treatment as the faction
  videos.
- **[SECONDARY DOCUMENT INSERTS]** — three brief cutaways to the
  original source documents (Video 01's orientation document, Video
  03's incident report and testimony card, Video 05's Ashfall file),
  each shown just long enough to place the exact quote being
  re-examined, then returning to the timeline.
- **[FINAL PAGE — BLANK ENTRY]** — the timeline's last page, showing
  1919 with nothing beside it, per the First Phantom Seed below.

No Full Frame moment this video, consistent with the complexity-2 rating
and the institutional-video pattern established in 08–10.

## On-Screen Text
No on-screen Archivist text during narration. Closing annotation card
only.

## Silence Note
[BEAT] — minimum two seconds.
[LONG BEAT] — minimum four seconds.

## First Phantom Seed
The blank 1919 entry is the central content of Section 4 — unlike most
prior seeds in this arc, this one is openly discussed by the Archivist
rather than passed over in silence, because it IS this video's stated
purpose. The restraint that applies here is different: **the narration
discusses the fact that the entry is blank, and reacts to it, but never
speculates about what the missing content might have been.** No guesses,
no theories floated and then walked back — the gap itself is the entire
statement.

## Image Transition Note
Document dissolves: three seconds in, three seconds out, house standard.
Secondary document inserts: same dissolve treatment, matching the
document-insert convention used in Videos 03/05/06 rather than the
harder cut language used for pure First Phantom seeds elsewhere — these
are call-backs the audience is meant to consciously recognize, not
hidden details.
