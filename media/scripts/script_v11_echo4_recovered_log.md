# Script Notes

## Runtime Target
6–8 minutes | No narration word count — this video runs on dialogue,
silence, and degraded audio instead of Archivist voiceover.

## Complexity
5/5 per video-pipeline.md — the highest in the arc.

## One Job
Pure recovered log. No narration. No document reading. Echo-4's final
hours. The Red Platform is reached. Nothing is explained.

## Format Break — Read This Before Anything Else
Every video so far has been built around the Archivist reading a
document on camera. **This video has no document and no Archivist
narration anywhere in its body.** The Archivist appears exactly once,
as silent on-screen text at the very end — nowhere else. If a draft of
this script drifts toward adding narration to "help the audience
understand what's happening," that instinct should be resisted. Per the
outline, nothing gets explained. The audience gets exactly what
Directorate archivists would have gotten: the recording, unedited, and
nothing more.

## Continuity Requirements — Read Before Writing Any Further Draft
This video pays off threads from Videos 02 and 06 and must not
contradict either:

- Video 06 established Echo-4's deployment orders precisely: *"contact-
  and-withdraw... confirm the entity, do not engage, report back within
  the hour."* This video should open consistent with those exact orders.
- Video 02's handwritten note — *"Do not board the final train"* — and
  its phantom train approach sound both recur here, for the third and
  final time. Per `docs/production/asset_sourcing_guide.md`, this must
  be **the exact same edited audio file** used in Videos 02 and 06 —
  do not regenerate or re-edit it.
- Transit ambient (Station 13 ambient) is also reused for a third time
  here — same base asset as Videos 02 and 06. The audience has heard it
  twice already; this is the payoff of that repetition, not a new sound.
- Neither Video 02 nor Video 06 ever states Echo-4's outcome. This video
  finally shows it — but "shows" does not mean "explains." The team
  reaches the Red Platform and the recording ends. No resolution, no
  confirmation of what the Red Platform actually is, no statement of
  what happened to the team afterward.
- **Reuse the Dispatcher voice character from the Short 01 script**
  (`media/shorts/shorts_launch_batch_01.md`) for this video's comms
  operator — same treatment: flat, institutional, slightly degraded
  radio processing. This is a deliberate continuity thread linking the
  shorts pipeline to this long-form payoff; do not create a new voice
  character for this role.

## Voice Direction
Three distinct Hunter voices, plus the reused Dispatcher voice:

- **ECHO-4 ACTUAL** (team lead) — measured, procedural, the calmest of
  the three even as things degrade. Trained to sound controlled. Should
  be the one who reaches the Red Platform.
- **ECHO-4 TWO** — younger-sounding, technical role, handles navigation
  and equipment. First to report something wrong, in clipped,
  professional language that slowly loses its composure.
- **ECHO-4 THREE** — quietest voice of the three, most affected by the
  phenomena, speaks the least but what she says lands hardest. Do not
  overwrite this role with more dialogue than necessary — restraint is
  the point.
- **DISPATCHER** — reused from Short 01, flat and institutional
  throughout, present only in the opening comms check and does not
  return once the team enters the tunnels (radio contact with the
  surface is lost, not maintained).

No character is named beyond call signs, consistent with house
convention of not naming individuals.

## Visual Approach
No document system at all in this video — the only video in the arc
without one.

- **[RECOVERED FOOTAGE BASE]** — near-black frame throughout, VHS
  timestamp burn-in bottom-left, progressive signal degradation visual
  effect that worsens across the runtime (tracking jitter increasing,
  color desaturating further, occasional total signal loss for a
  fraction of a second).
- **[RECOVERED PHOTOGRAPH — FULL FRAME]** — the video's single generated
  image, held for exactly three seconds during the Red Platform section.
  See First Phantom Seed below.

## On-Screen Text
The pre-roll file label card and the closing annotation are the ONLY
on-screen text in this video — no other captions, no timestamps beyond
the VHS burn-in (which is diegetic, not Archivist text).

## Silence Note
This video uses more planned silence than any other video in the arc.
[BEAT] = minimum two seconds. [LONG BEAT] = minimum four seconds.
[DEAD AIR] = a new marker for this video only — five to eight seconds
of near-total silence (ambient only, no dialogue, no comms crackle) —
used at moments where lesser videos would have filled the space. Do not
compress [DEAD AIR] markers under any circumstance; they are the
backbone of this video's tension.

## First Phantom Seed — Read Carefully Before Building the Photograph Asset
During the Red Platform section, a single recovered photograph appears
on screen for exactly three seconds. It shows Echo-4 Actual reaching the
platform, damaged/degraded as a practical recovered print. **In the
background, out of focus, a figure is visible — not Echo-4 Two or
Three, not HH-013 (The Conductor).** The figure is never referenced in
dialogue, never explained, never revisited. Camera does not zoom or
push in — it holds the frame naturally and moves on, exactly as
described in the outline. Whoever builds this asset should know (for
internal continuity reference only, never stated in the video itself)
that this is understood in canon as an oblique glimpse of HH-021, The
First Phantom — per that entity's own file, it is "referenced obliquely
in Station 13 recovered recordings." This video is that reference. It
must remain exactly as oblique as the entity file requires — no
silhouette detail beyond an indistinct human shape at the edge of
resolution.

## Image Transition Note
No dissolves in this video — everything is hard cuts and static-burst
transitions, consistent with degraded found footage rather than
document-reading. The one exception is the recovered photograph, which
should flash in abruptly (a single frame of static immediately
preceding it) and cut out the same way — no dissolve either direction.
