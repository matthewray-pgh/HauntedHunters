# Script Notes

## Runtime Target
8–10 minutes | ~885–985 words of narration

*(Expanded from the original 6–7 min / ~650–750 words per the Video 05
script-expansion patch — ~235 words of new narration added across four
insertion points in Sections 1, 2, 3 and Current Status. Estimated
runtime after expansion ~7:45–8:30 at existing pacing — the tightest
fit of the expanded scripts; if it still reads short after BEAT tuning,
add another short beat inside Insertion 4 (the "static recedes" close).
No new visual assets, no third redaction bar — still exactly two — and
all inserts sit inside the existing static-escalation curve.)*

## Complexity
4/5 per video-pipeline.md — the highest complexity in the arc so far.
The jump from Video 04's restraint is intentional: this is the pivot
video. Videos 01–04 are external horror — something out there, something
that happened to people. This is the first internal horror video — the
Directorate did this to itself, on purpose, and someone signed off on it.
The production should feel that shift: more visual instability, less
composure than any video before it.

## One Job
Shift from external horror to internal horror. The Directorate caused
something. Someone approved it. Do not let the spectacle of the
degradation effects bury that point — the horror here is bureaucratic
before it is architectural.

## Voice Direction
Same Archivist treatment as Videos 01–04 — ElevenLabs, tape recorder
playback, slight wow and flutter, subtle hiss, narrow frequency range.
This file should carry more restrained anger than any prior video — not
fear. The Archivist is not scared of Ashfall. The Archivist is frustrated
by it. That's a different color of delivery than Video 03 or 04 and
should be directed accordingly in ElevenLabs settings if manual emotion
tags are available.

## Visual Approach
Two visual systems, plus a continuous effect that escalates across the
entire video rather than appearing in discrete inserts:

- **[DOCUMENT]** — the Ashfall project file. Same base treatment as
  Videos 01–04, but **already heavily redacted from the moment it
  appears** — thick black bars over portions of typed text, unlike any
  prior document in this arc, which have all been legible until a
  specific narrative beat withheld something.
- **[FACILITY FOOTAGE]** — the Ashfall Research Facility corridor image
  (prompt already exists in `docs/visual-style-guide.md`), used as this
  video's Full Frame moment.
- **[PROGRESSIVE STATIC]** — not a separate visual system, but a
  continuous overlay treatment. Static interference and VHS tracking
  jitter begin faint at video open and intensify steadily through the
  Activation Event and Recovery Team Reports sections, peaking during
  the Full Frame corridor hold, then partially recede (never fully clear)
  for Current Status and the closing annotation. This is the one video
  in the arc where the image itself visibly destabilizes as the subject
  matter destabilizes — do not use this technique again elsewhere in
  the arc, it should stay unique to Ashfall.

**[LOCATION INSERT — FACILITY MAP]** used once, at the end, for the First
Phantom seed — see Section 4 below.

## On-Screen Text
No on-screen Archivist text during narration. Closing annotation card
only, per house convention. The redaction bars are diegetic (they belong
to the document, not to the Archivist) — do not treat them as Archivist
text.

## Silence Note
[BEAT] — minimum two seconds.
[LONG BEAT] — minimum four seconds.
Given the added visual noise in this video, resist the urge to compress
silences to "make room" for the static effects. The silences need to
hold their full length precisely because the image is unstable — the
quiet is what lets the wrongness register instead of just reading as
technical difficulty.

## Real-Time Redaction Bar Mechanic (New This Video)
At two points in the document sections, a black redaction bar animates
across a line of visible text while the camera holds on it — the text is
legible for roughly one second, then covered, live, as if something is
still actively withholding it. This is distinct from every prior video's
redaction, which has always been static (already blacked out, never
watched happening). Use sparingly — twice total in this script, marked
below. Overuse turns a genuinely unsettling effect into a gimmick.

## Image Transition Note
Document dissolves: three seconds in, three seconds out, house standard.
Facility footage (Full Frame): dissolve in, but do NOT dissolve out
cleanly — cut out through a static burst instead, consistent with the
progressive-static system reaching its peak at that moment. Facility map
insert: hard cut in, six-second silent hold, hard cut out — no dissolve,
no narration bridging in or out (see Section 4 notes).
