# Script Notes

## Runtime Target
8–10 minutes | ~770–900 words of narration

*(Expanded from the original 6–7 min / ~650–780 words per the Video 10
script-expansion patch — ~120 words of new narration across two
insertion points, both extending Sections 1 and 2. Deliberately the
smallest expansion of the trilogy: much of this video's runtime is
protected by the "implied, never stated" thinness of its endgame
section, which is NOT filled in. Insertion 2 is the closest the arc
comes to the "one founder" observation and stops at "the same hand" —
if on review it reads as too close to an explicit reveal, cut it
entirely and keep only Insertion 1. Estimated runtime after expansion
~7:15–8:00, helped further by the "small hesitations" pacing direction.
Shared-signatory seed, third/final appearance, spec unchanged.)*

## Complexity
2/5 per video-pipeline.md.

## One Job
Introduce the most unsettling faction — they think humanity should adapt
to the Veil. First use of the bruised purple visual treatment (Veil-
adjacent contamination) on an institutional document. The signatory
appears for the third time — all three factions, one author.

## Continuity Requirements — Read Before Writing Any Further Draft
This video closes out the three-part signatory thread started in
Video 08 and continued in Video 09. Handle the close carefully:

- **Signatory spec must match Videos 08 and 09 exactly** — same
  position (bottom third of the charter page, beneath the founding
  declaration, left-aligned with the paragraph margin), same width
  (~90px, two-word-name equivalent), same color (`#5A6470`, pending
  style guide confirmation, must match what was actually used in
  Videos 08 and 09's production, not just this script's description).
- **"No comment," per `first-phantom-thread-seed-map.md`.** The seed
  map is explicit: *"One author founded all three opposing factions. No
  comment. The documents simply share this detail."* The Archivist may
  notice the pattern and react to it emotionally — this video's
  annotation is specified as the most agitated of the three — but must
  **not state outright that one person founded all three factions.**
  The visual recognition (three identical redactions across three
  videos) is the reveal. The narration gets close to it and stops short.
  This is a harder needle to thread than Video 09's restraint, because
  this is the third appearance and the temptation to finally say it out
  loud will be strong. Resist it.
- The purple used here (`#5F4B6E`) is the same hex as Video 07's
  Obsidian classification stamp. This is intentional — the same color
  now appearing on an ordinary internal document, rather than on an
  entity file, is what should read as wrong. Do not treat this as a
  coincidence to smooth over; it is the visual argument of the video.

## Voice Direction
Same Archivist treatment as Videos 01–09 — ElevenLabs, tape recorder
playback, slight wow and flutter, subtle hiss, narrow frequency range.
This is the first time in the arc the Archivist should sound genuinely
unsettled rather than restrained, frustrated, or clinical. Not
frightened in the entity-file sense — agitated, like someone reading a
document that keeps almost saying something they don't want confirmed.
Direct ElevenLabs toward a slightly less even pace than prior videos if
manual pacing control is available — small hesitations are appropriate
here for the first time in the arc.

## Visual Approach
Same structural approach as Videos 08 and 09 — single document system,
no Midjourney-generated entity or location imagery — plus one new
element unique to this video:

- **[DOCUMENT]** — the Threshold Initiative internal intelligence
  assessment. Same crisp, current-document treatment as Videos 08–09,
  but with the bruised purple `#5F4B6E` treatment described below rather
  than a clean accent color.
- **[VEIL CONTAMINATION VISUAL ELEMENT]** — new this video. Not a
  Midjourney asset — a CapCut-built effect: a faint, slowly spreading
  purple discoloration across the document's paper texture itself,
  as though the page were bruised or water-damaged from beneath the
  surface rather than aged from without. Starts nearly invisible at
  document-open, spreads almost imperceptibly slower than Video 05's
  progressive static (this is subtler, not louder), never resolves into
  a shape or pattern — it should read as contamination, not decoration.
- **[CHARTER INSERT]** — the Threshold Initiative's founding charter,
  carrying the shared redacted-signatory seed's third and final
  appearance, spec locked to Videos 08 and 09 exactly.

No Full Frame moment this video, consistent with Videos 08–09 and the
complexity-2 rating.

## On-Screen Text
No on-screen Archivist text during narration. Closing annotation card
only.
