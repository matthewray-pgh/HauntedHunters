# Obsidian Frequency — Halloween 2026 Release Calendar

## Active Push: Aug 25 – Nov 17, 2026

This is the dated source of truth for the launch-through-Arc-1-completion
push. It supersedes the steady-state cadence in `release-strategy.md` and
`../../shorts/short_form_content_system.md` for this window only — both of
those documents point back here for exact dates and resume their default
guidance after Video 13.

**Why this exists:** Arc 1 (13 episodes) is being told on a fixed weekly
long-form cadence, with shorts ramping alongside, specifically to build
audience during the Halloween season while horror content has real seasonal
search and discovery advantage. This is a deliberate, temporary departure
from the channel's steady-state "irregular release, quality over schedule"
identity — see `release-strategy.md` Philosophy section for the full
reasoning and what resumes after this push ends.

**The real constraint at this pace is scripting, not production.** Scripts
for Videos 01–03 are done. Every video after that needs a finished script
roughly 10 days ahead of its release date or the whole cadence collapses.
The "Script needed by" column below is the actual thing to track.
